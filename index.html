GOOD TO KNOW

A few moving questions.

Do I need to leave everything outside?

No. Keep items dry and secure until the team arrives. For ready-to-collect service, have them packed and ready at an accessible ground-floor entrance. Choose indoor help if items need carrying from rooms or upstairs.

Why do you ask about stairs and parking?

They affect how long the work takes and the team needed. Checking both addresses helps us agree a realistic price before you book.

Can you collect something I bought online?

Yes—use the collection and delivery option and add a listing link or photos. Please check the seller’s access and collection arrangements too. If you do not know, select “Not sure”.

What if my items are unusually heavy?

Tell us about the item and include dimensions or weight if known. We review specialist equipment, narrow access and unusually heavy items before accepting the job.

Will I get an instant price?

We provide a reviewed quote. We check the details and agree the price before booking. Extra items or changed requirements are agreed before additional work starts.
